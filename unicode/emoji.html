Emoji

An emoji can be either a single character or sequence of codepoints consisting of a base character with modifiers.

    Presentation Style

    Unqualified emoji can be displayed in either text or emoji styles. The U+FE0F (VS-16) codepoint switches to the "emoji" style, while U+FE0E (VS-15) selects "text".
