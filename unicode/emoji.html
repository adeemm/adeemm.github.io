Presentation Style

219 "unqualified" emoji can be displayed in either "text" or "emoji" styles. The U+FE0F (VS-16) codepoint switches to the "emoji" style, while U+FE0E (VS-15) selects "text".

If a style is unsupported, the selector is ignored.
